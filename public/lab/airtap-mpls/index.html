01 · the path

One VRF per customer, from the rig to their data center

Offshore rigs landed on Cisco 7200s over point-to-point microwave. From there the traffic crossed Time Warner and AT&T fiber MPLS into 7200s running BGP and EIGRP. Each customer stayed in its own VRF — amber for Customer A, cyan for Customer B — and that VRF continued to the customer data center. Two egress points carried multihomed BGP out to the internet.
