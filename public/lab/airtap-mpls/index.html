01 · the path

One VRF per customer, from the rig to their data center

Three offshore rigs sit in the Gulf, each with a coverage ring. The rings cross, so the overlap between rigs is visible. Each rig comes ashore on a straight point-to-point microwave shot to a Cisco 7200: the Texas shore, the central tip of the coast, and a landing further east. All three run straight north to one inland data center, where BGP and EIGRP keep Customer A and Customer B in their own VRFs. Two egress peers in the cloud north of the data center carry multihomed BGP.
